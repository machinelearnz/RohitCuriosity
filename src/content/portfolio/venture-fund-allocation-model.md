Predicting venture fund performance across multiple market cycles requires moving beyond rigid static spreadsheets.

**VentureSim** empowers general partners and emerging fund managers to model dynamic portfolio outcomes under volatile valuation distributions and multi-round dilution.

---

## ⚙️ Key Capabilities
- **Monte Carlo Liquidity Modeling:** Simulates power-law fund returns across 10,000 distinct exit probability paths.
- **Pro-Rata Reserve Strategy Optimization:** Determines the exact capital reserve ratio necessary to defend ownership in breakout series-B winners.
- **Waterfall Distribution Visualizer:** Interactive graphical waterfall breakdown for LPs and GPs illustrating carried interest hurdles.
