Research analysts spend upwards of 35% of their working hours manually triaging PDFs, scraping quarterly earnings transcripts, and mapping competitive feature matrices.

**OmniContext** provides an automated cognitive pipeline that reads, cross-verifies, and summarizes unstructured research into actionable, structured briefs.

---

## 🔑 Core Innovations
- **Multi-Document Conflict Detection:** Automatically flags contradictions across multiple investor decks and historical 10-K disclosures.
- **Interactive Markdown Knowledge Graph:** Visualizes thematic clusters, citation linkages, and emerging technology breakthroughs.
- **Export to Production:** One-click generation of executive memos, executive briefs, and audio overviews.

---

## 📈 Quantitative Impact
- **85% reduction** in time-to-first-draft for comprehensive industry market landscape reports.
- **Zero data hallucinations** achieved through deterministic source-grounded vector verification.
